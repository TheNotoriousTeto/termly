**Creating an App for iPhones**
I grant you access to whatever apps/utilities that you might need, including Terminal and Xcode. Use them to implement the app described below

Description:

Target Audience:
the app is designed for undergraduate students as the main target, there are multiple sections for the app, explained below.

First Section:
first section has the GPA calculator, it has a semester-GPA, and over-all-GPA, as in you enter the section, you have mini subsections which are the semesters you've gone through e.g. "2025 - Winter", or "2023 - Spring", each with the total credit points of the semester and the average GPA for that semester, each semester subsection is going to be a rectangle-like shape (with round corners) and each line would have 2 semesters. in that page, and above every subsection, the person can see his GPA over all the semesters, like the total GPA.
when you enter each semester subsection in that page, you'll see the courses in the same way/aesthetics the semesters looked, each course will have the name instead of the semester, for example instead of "2025 Winter" there will be "Calculus 1 - nnnnnnnn" where "nnnnnnnn" is the course's number (always 8 digits long and numbers only), instead of total credits for that semester you'll see the course's credit points, and instead of the GPA of that semester you'll see the grade of that course.
obviously the user inserts the courses and the semesters in both pages, like there should be "Add" or "+" button in each page so that the person can add a course/semester, and on the course/semester, there will be a " ⃛" button (three dots, meaning menu), where if a person press on that it shows them two options, "Edit" and "Delete", keeping delete in red for the aesthetics. when someone presses "Delete" it asks if they're sure. when someone presses "Edit" it shows what they have inserted before they clicked "Add Course/Semester" and they can edit, but now instead of "Add Course/Semester" it says "Save".
when the user presses on the add button for a semester, it only asks for the year, and the person gets to choose from a list between "Winter", "Spring", or "Summer".
when the user presses on the add button for a course, it shows them a form-like thing they need to fill, with 4 sections; "Course Number", then "Course Name", then "Credit Points", then "Grade", and under the last section there will be a "Add Course" button once they finish that adds whatever they filled. after the user adds the rectangle of the course will have "Course Name - Course Number" as header, taking the value the user filled, then with dimmer smaller font there will be "Credit Points: x" and under it "Grade: y" where x is the value they wrote in the "Credit Points" section and y is what they wrote in "Grade" section.
Important Note, the "Credit Points" shows a list, from "Binary" (equivalently 0) to 6, only multiples of 0.5, meaning they can't choose a course with higher credit points than 6, nor courses with credit points of 2.6, and for courses that they mark as "Binary", they don't get added into the GPA, not the semester's GPA nor the Total GPA, only there for keeping track.
the app should prevent adding two exact same semesters, like if someone adds a "2025 - Winter" semester and tries to add another "2025 - Winter" semester, it'd say something like "Semester Already Exists".
the app checks courses duplication via the course number, meaning if someone tries to insert a new course, uses a number that already exists in any other semester, it'd say "Course Already Exists in xxx" where instead of xxx it'd tell them which semester the course exists in.
in the GPA page (the one including the courses), the header will be "Total GPA: N" where N is the total GPA calculated from courses of all semesters, and N is up to 2 digits after the point, no matter what the number is, we round up only for 8 or 9, examples: 88.293 → 88.29, 78.447 → 78.44, 91.549 → 91.55, 63.278→ 63.28. next to the total GPA, it'd show "Total Credit Points: M" where M is the sum of all credit points of all semesters.
inside each semester's page, the header will be "Semester's GPA: N" where N is the GPA of the semester the user's entered based on the courses they inserted, and the same rule apply, N is up to 2 digits after the point, no matter what the number is, we round up only for 8 or 9, examples: 88.293 → 88.29, 78.447 → 78.44, 91.549 → 91.55, 63.278→ 63.28. next to the semester's GPA, it'd show "Semester's Credit Points: M" where M is the sum of all credit points of this selected semester.
and that for now is the first section.

Second Section:
second section is like a task/time manager for the user, they insert what they wanna do, and they get to write what they want and there are no restrictions, for example they can write "calc 1 homework" or "doctor's appointment at 2pm", and they choose to put a time for "Finish Before", and there are an option to choose between "none" "important" and "very important", after they insert, they'd be listed based on importance, the "vert important" ones would have ‼️ next to them, the "important" ones would have ❗️ next to them, and the "none" wouldn't have anything.
for each one in the list, there will be an empty box, when the finish, the press on it and it shows a V tick meaning they finished it, if they press again it goes back to blank so that if they pressed it on accident it doesn't prevent them from undoing that. and the user can fill up the "to do" task up to a week later, meaning on Mondays they can already decide/insert what to do up for the next Sunday, on Thursdays the same for up to the next Wednesday, and so on.
for it to be more "rewarding", the person can give each task they add a number, as points, and as they finish it, it'll add the percentage of that number of points out of all the points for this day's task, to encourage the progress.
for each task, there will also be a " ⃛" button (three dots, like a menu), also showing "Edit" or "Delete" in the same aesthetics for the courses and semesters. when someone presses "Delete" it asks if they're sure. when someone presses "Edit" it shows what they have inserted before they clicked "Add Task" and they can edit, but now instead of "Add Task" it says "Save".
in short, when they enter the "Tasks" or "Task Manager" (or whatever the best name is) section, there will be an option to choose from "Today" and up to <next week - 1 day> (like I've explained), when they enter Today's section, there will be the tasks for the day, from the most important to the least, 2 tasks with the same importance would be listed from the highest points to the lowest.
